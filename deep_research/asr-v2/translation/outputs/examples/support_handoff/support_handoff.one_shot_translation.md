# Segment-Preserving Translation

- Example: `support_handoff`
- Variant: `one_shot_translation`
- Language Pair: `zh -> en`
- Segments: `3`

- [0000.00-0003.00] **SPEAKER_00**: The client said they will confirm the contract again at 10 a.m. tomorrow.
- [0003.10-0004.90] **SPEAKER_01**: I'll prepare the English version first.
- [0005.00-0006.90] **SPEAKER_00**: Remember to attach the old quote as well.
